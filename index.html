CaseLog

Your logbook lives on this iPhone only. Add CaseLog to your Home Screen first, so your cases are kept inside the app.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll the list if you don't see it.
  3. Open CaseLog from your Home Screen and log from there.

Cases saved in a Safari tab stay in Safari and don't appear in the Home Screen app.
